# Sustainable Development Goal alignment

## Assessment

The project has a **potential enabling relationship** to SDG 7, Affordable and Clean Energy, specifically Target 7.3. The UN defines Target 7.3 as doubling the global rate of improvement in energy efficiency by 2030; its indicator 7.3.1 measures primary energy intensity relative to GDP ([UN Goal 7](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal7), [UN indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Goal=7)).

This repository does not calculate that indicator, measure energy-efficiency improvement, or evaluate an intervention. Clustering alone is not evidence of SDG impact.

| Claim level | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Demonstrated impact | None. No deployment or intervention outcome is measured. |
| Technical contribution | The software groups synthetic and selected measured profiles by temporal behavior and visualizes load-shape differences. |
| Potential relevance | If validated with representative utility data and followed by evaluated interventions, segmentation could inform further energy-efficiency research. This is a hypothesis, not a result. |
| Possible future indicators | For an intervention study: metered energy per participant before/after, a preregistered comparison group, and a clearly defined efficiency outcome. These are not current project metrics. |
| Limitations | The primary labels are synthetic; the UCI clients lack behavioral labels; the London analysis is a restricted subset; the load-shift scenarios are counterfactual. |

No contribution to SDG 9, 11, 12, or 13 is claimed from the current evidence. Revisit this assessment only after a measured deployment or another directly relevant study.
